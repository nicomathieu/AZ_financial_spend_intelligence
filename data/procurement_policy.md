# Global Procurement & Spend Policy (Excerpt)
**Document ID:** FIN-POL-014 · **Version:** 3.2 · **Effective:** 01-Jan-2025 · **Owner:** Global Finance, Procurement Excellence

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy governs the procurement of goods and services across all enabling functions. It applies to all purchases regardless of funding source, excluding clinical trial site payments (covered by FIN-POL-021).

## 2. Purchase Order Requirements
2.1 All purchases of goods or services with a value of **EUR 1,000 or above** require an approved Purchase Order (PO) issued **before** the supplier begins work or ships goods.

2.2 Invoices received without a valid PO reference ("maverick spend") above the EUR 1,000 threshold must be flagged to the Cost Center owner and reported quarterly to the Finance Controls Committee.

2.3 Recurring services (e.g., cloud hosting, facilities) may operate under a blanket PO, renewed annually.

## 3. Approval Thresholds
| Invoice / PO Value (EUR) | Required Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to 5,000 | Cost Center Owner |
| 5,000.01 – 50,000 | Department Head |
| 50,000.01 – 250,000 | Finance Director |
| Above 250,000 | CFO + Procurement Excellence review |

3.1 Approval is evidenced by the `approved_by` field in the invoice processing system. Invoices lacking an approver record must be held and may not be paid.

## 4. Vendor Management
4.1 Payments may only be made to vendors with **ACTIVE** status in the Vendor Master. Invoices from vendors with status **ON_HOLD** or **INACTIVE** must be escalated to Procurement Excellence before processing.

4.2 New vendors must complete due diligence (financial, compliance, and where applicable, GxP qualification) before first payment.

4.3 Vendor master data changes (bank details, legal name) require dual approval and are subject to fraud-prevention callback verification.

## 5. Currency & Payment Terms
5.1 The Group reporting currency is **EUR**. Invoices in other currencies are converted at the ECB reference rate on the invoice date for reporting purposes.

5.2 Standard payment terms are **60 days net** from invoice receipt. Early-payment discounts above 2% require Treasury approval.

## 6. Credit Notes & Negative Amounts
6.1 Negative invoice amounts must be processed as credit notes and matched against the original invoice. Unmatched credit notes older than 90 days are escalated to the Finance Director.

## 7. Segregation of Duties
7.1 The person approving an invoice may not be the requester of the underlying purchase.

7.2 Self-approval is prohibited at all thresholds.

## 8. Audit & Records
8.1 All procurement records, including invoices, POs, and approval evidence, are retained for **10 years** in compliance with applicable financial regulations.

8.2 Data supporting financial reporting is subject to internal controls testing (SOX-equivalent). Any automated system that transforms or aggregates this data must maintain a traceable lineage from source to report.

## 9. Exceptions
9.1 Emergency purchases (patient safety, business continuity) may bypass the PO requirement with retrospective approval within 5 business days, documented via the Exception Register.
